SERVICE SCOPE

Match bin capacity and service access to the washroom.

Scope options to discuss

  • Sanitary-bin locations and unit requirements
  • Agreed exchange or servicing tasks
  • Waste-handling and collection responsibilities
  • Service-frequency and replenishment requirements

Agree these items separately

Unit purchase, installation, general washroom cleaning and other waste streams should be itemised rather than assumed included.

Your written quote defines the included tasks, exclusions and responsibilities.

ACCESS & SCHEDULING

Fit the work around your facility.

Timing and access

Identify washroom use, opening hours and any privacy or escort requirements. Provide peak occupancy periods and the access available to service each unit.

Before the site assessment

Send washroom and bin counts, existing unit details and preferred service arrangement. Include storage, collection access and responsibility for consumables.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote.

Can existing sanitary bins be used?

Provide the model, condition and current servicing arrangement. Compatibility with the proposed collection or exchange approach needs confirmation.

Is this part of a janitorial contract?

It can be included as a separate requested item. State who supplies units, services them and arranges the relevant waste handling.
