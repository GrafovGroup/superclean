YOUR PROPERTY

Define the site

For a North York office, commercial unit or managed property, identify the tenant space and the areas controlled by building management. A suite-level request should name any shared corridors, plant rooms or exterior areas separately. Send material and coating details, photographs and previous cleaning history. Mark damaged areas, water access, drainage and planting around the work zone.

Access and operations

Identify public access, vehicle movements and nearby work. Weather, application requirements and protection arrangements affect the washing window. List security-desk requirements, elevator access and the person who can release restricted rooms. For multiple floors or units, provide a separate access contact and work window where they differ.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your North York request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Specified façade and exterior surfaces
  • Soiling and existing-damage assessment
  • Product and method compatibility review
  • Protection of planting, fixtures and adjacent finishes

Separate scope decisions

Repairs, recoating, deep staining, high-access equipment and specialist residues require separate scope decisions. Lower pressure does not establish suitability for every finish.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can delicate exterior finishes be assessed?

Provide the material and care instructions where available. Suitability should be reviewed before a product or pressure approach is selected.

Is a low-pressure wash the same as a general power wash?

State the finish and cleaning objective. The proposed method, products, protection and expected result should be described in the agreed scope.

Can a North York tenant arrange work in shared building areas?

Identify which spaces the tenant controls and which require the owner or manager’s approval. A request can include both, but permissions, access contacts and responsibilities must be clear for each area.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
