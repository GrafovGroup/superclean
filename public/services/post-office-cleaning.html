Post Office Cleaning

Cleaning plans for postal counters, staff areas and sorting facilities.

SERVICE SCOPE

Protect secure operations while defining the daily routine.

Scope options to discuss

  • Public counters and accessible customer areas
  • Staff amenities and washrooms
  • Agreed floors and circulation routes
  • Waste collection to designated points

Agree these items separately

Mail handling, sorting machinery, secure storage, consumables and specialist floor treatments are separate responsibilities.

Your written quote defines the included tasks, exclusions and responsibilities.

ACCESS & SCHEDULING

Fit the work around your facility.

Timing and access

Share opening hours, parcel movements and access procedures. Identify zones that remain occupied or require an authorised escort during cleaning.

Before the site assessment

Provide floor area, counter and washroom counts, restricted-zone boundaries and your existing checklist. Name the person who approves contractor access.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote.

Can a sorting area be included?

List accessible areas and tasks separately from mail-handling equipment and secure storage. The operator must define permissions and any escort requirements.

Should public and staff areas use the same schedule?

Describe footfall and occupancy for each zone. Customer counters, staff rooms and secure operational areas may need different timing and frequencies.
