Window Glazing

Window glazing enquiries for commercial properties and managed buildings.

Request a quote ↗

SERVICE SCOPE

Describe the glass and the repair or replacement requirement.

Scope options to discuss

  • Glass, frame and opening identification
  • Damage and existing-condition review
  • Access and handling requirements
  • Repair or replacement options for assessment

Agree these items separately

Window cleaning, frame restoration, structural alterations and emergency attendance are separate enquiries. Specifications and qualified installation arrangements require confirmation.

Your written quote defines the included tasks, exclusions and responsibilities.

ACCESS & SCHEDULING

Fit the work around your facility.

Timing and access

Share tenant hours, security requirements and access to both sides of the opening. Procurement, equipment access and temporary protection affect the work sequence.

Before the site assessment

Send photographs, approximate dimensions and any existing glass specifications. Identify height, access restrictions and the property contact responsible for the opening.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote.

Should cleaning and glazing be requested separately?

Yes. Describe removable marks separately from cracks, failed units or damaged frames so the right assessment and scope can be arranged.

Can a replacement be specified from a photograph?

Photographs help identify the enquiry, but dimensions, specifications and site conditions need confirmation before a replacement is agreed.
