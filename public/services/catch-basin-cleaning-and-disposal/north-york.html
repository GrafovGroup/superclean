Define the site

For a North York office, commercial unit or managed property, identify the tenant space and the areas controlled by building management. A suite-level request should name any shared corridors, plant rooms or exterior areas separately. Send a site plan with basin numbers, previous service records and known contamination concerns. Include approach dimensions and any restrictions on opening covers.

Access and operations

Agree the order in which parking or loading zones can be cleared. Identify delivery peaks, cover access restrictions and any need for property-management coordination. List security-desk requirements, elevator access and the person who can release restricted rooms. For multiple floors or units, provide a separate access contact and work window where they differ.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can basin servicing be combined with parking-lot cleaning?

List the basins and surface-cleaning zones separately. Access may be coordinated, but equipment, waste and completion requirements differ.

What if the sediment may be contaminated?

Provide known history and existing reports. Material assessment and appropriate handling and disposal arrangements must be reviewed before the work is scoped.

Can a North York tenant arrange work in shared building areas?

Identify which spaces the tenant controls and which require the owner or manager’s approval. A request can include both, but permissions, access contacts and responsibilities must be clear for each area.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
