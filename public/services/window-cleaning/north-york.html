Window cleaning in North York

Commercial window cleaning in North York. Compare interior glazing, storefront glass and exterior-access requirements for an accurately scoped quote.

YOUR PROPERTY

Distinguish reachable glass from elevated exterior work.

Define the site

For a North York building, count storefront panes, interior partitions and upper-storey exterior windows separately. A ground-floor glass-cleaning request does not establish safe access to the rest of the façade. Include window dimensions, frame materials and any restrictions on opening or accessing the glass.

Access and operations

Show the ground area below the windows, including awnings, parked vehicles and public entrances. State whether interior access is available through occupied suites and who can approve exterior equipment access. Height, weather and access method must be assessed together.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your North York request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Interior glazing and entrance doors
  • Exterior panes and storefront glass
  • Frames, sills and tracks when requested
  • Accessible partitions and internal glass features

Separate scope decisions

High-access equipment, construction residue, hard-water deposits, screens and glazing repairs require separate assessment. A standard glass-cleaning scope should not imply all of these are included.

PREPARE YOUR QUOTE

Send the details that change the plan.

  • Glass counts or approximate area by elevation
  • Interior and exterior scope listed separately
  • Photographs showing height and ground-level obstructions
  • Paint, adhesive, mineral deposits or damaged glass identified separately

Include your full North York address, target dates and site contact. Availability, the method, included tasks and any specialist requirements are confirmed for the property before work is arranged.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can a storefront quote include glass above the entrance canopy?

Identify the canopy and upper glass separately with photographs. Reach, equipment positioning and pedestrian access need assessment before that area is included.

Does routine window cleaning remove construction residue?

Paint, adhesive, mortar and other construction residue require separate review. The glass and coating must be identified before choosing a method, and some marks may be damage rather than removable soil.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
