Managed-property & tenant-turnover cleaning

Turnover and common-area cleaning enquiries for property managers, landlords and professionally managed residential portfolios.

Request a quote ↗

SERVICE SCOPE

A practical handover scope for property managers.

Scope options to discuss

  • Vacant-unit rooms and accessible surfaces
  • Kitchens and bathrooms as specified
  • Floor care matched to the existing finishes
  • Shared entrances or amenities when separately listed

Agree these items separately

Tenant belongings, junk removal, pests, hazardous materials, repairs and restoration require separate assessment. Appliances, cupboards, balconies and interior windows should be itemised.

Your written quote defines the included tasks, exclusions and responsibilities.

ACCESS & SCHEDULING

Fit the work around your facility.

Timing and access

Work back from the next tenant’s access date. Confirm that trades and move-out activity are complete before the final clean, and allow for any agreed drying time.

Before the site assessment

Share unit count, approximate size, condition photos and your move-in checklist. Include key collection, parking, elevator bookings and the property manager’s handover contact.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote.

Is this intended for property portfolios?

This enquiry is for landlords and property managers arranging vacant-unit turnovers or common-area cleaning in professionally managed properties.

Are appliance interiors included?

List ovens, refrigerators and other appliances individually, along with their condition and whether they will be empty and accessible.

Can several turnovers be coordinated?

Provide each address, unit number and handover date. Scope, condition and availability need to be assessed for each unit.
