SERVICE SCOPE

Identify the activity and the building areas involved.

Scope options to discuss

  • Species information and signs-of-entry review
  • Affected and adjoining area assessment
  • Qualified removal or exclusion requirements
  • Follow-up and reporting objectives

Agree these items separately

Building repairs, contamination cleanup, pest treatment and continuing exclusion work require separate scopes. Local wildlife requirements and specialist arrangements need review.

Your written quote defines the included tasks, exclusions and responsibilities.

ACCESS & SCHEDULING

Fit the work around your facility.

Timing and access

Describe occupancy, tenant access and any active operational areas. The appropriate provider must assess the work sequence and restrictions for the identified animal and site.

Before the site assessment

Send observations and photographs taken from a safe distance, together with suspected entry locations and dates. Provide existing reports and the property manager’s contact.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote.

Does an assessment automatically include sealing entry points?

List exclusion and building repairs separately. The provider must review the animal, entry route and suitable sequence before those tasks are agreed.

Can a managed building request help for several affected units?

Identify each unit and shared area, with access contacts and observed activity. Permissions and the scope need to be clear for every location.
