Define the site

For a Vaughan commercial unit or managed property, distinguish the tenant’s work area from shared parking, entrances and service spaces. Identify the owner or manager responsible for common property before requesting a combined scope. Send mat count, dimensions, material and backing details. Include condition photographs, cleaning history and responsibility for removing and reinstalling each mat.

Access and operations

Identify quiet entrance periods and whether spare mats are available. Agree temporary access and the condition required before mats return to pedestrian use. LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can fixed entrance matting be included?

Provide the system details and photographs. Fixed matting, loose mats and recessed entrance systems can have different access and treatment requirements.

Does cleaning include exchanging rental mats?

State whether you want cleaning, exchange or replacement. An existing rental arrangement should be identified before a separate service scope is proposed.

Can several Vaughan commercial units be included in one enquiry?

List each unit, task and access contact, including the manager responsible for shared areas. A combined request should preserve the individual scope, permissions and availability assessment for every property.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
