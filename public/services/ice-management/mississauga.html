Define the site

For a Mississauga business property, separate public and office areas from storage, loading and active operations. Access and operations

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can treatments be specified for delicate paving?

Provide finish details and manufacturer restrictions. Product suitability and expected effects should be reviewed before a treatment is agreed.

Who monitors changing conditions?

Define that responsibility in the service agreement, together with reporting, treatment triggers and any arrangements for additional attendance.

Can work be planned around deliveries at a Mississauga facility?

Provide delivery times, loading-door locations and the route to the proposed work area. Scope and timing can then be assessed around the areas the operator can release; no access arrangement is assumed in advance.
