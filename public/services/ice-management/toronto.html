Define the site

For a Toronto property, distinguish the work area from neighbouring premises, shared entrances and public routes. Provide the site contact who can approve access to each affected area. Send a site map, surface finishes, product restrictions and previous problem areas. Mark drainage, shaded routes and the person who communicates changing conditions.

Access and operations

Identify opening hours, high-use periods and known refreezing locations. Monitoring responsibility and attendance arrangements must be agreed for the property. Include loading or service-elevator bookings, tenant restrictions and any shared laneway access. Give the work-site address even when the request comes from a separate management office.

What access details help with a Toronto service quote?

Include the property address, permitted work hours, loading arrangements and the areas controlled by tenants or building management. Identify public routes and neighbouring spaces that the proposed work could affect.
