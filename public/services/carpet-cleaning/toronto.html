YOUR PROPERTY

Plan around the carpet condition and the next time it will be used.

Define the site

For a Toronto property, distinguish the work area from neighbouring premises, shared entrances and public routes. Provide the site contact who can approve access to each affected area. Share carpet type if known, approximate area, stain photographs and prior treatments. Identify furniture that must remain in place and the latest time the space can reopen.

Access and operations

Allow for cleaning, drying and reopening. Fibre, backing, ventilation, soil level and the selected method affect the return-to-use plan; a fixed drying time should be agreed only after assessment. Include loading or service-elevator bookings, tenant restrictions and any shared laneway access. Give the work-site address even when the request comes from a separate management office.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Toronto request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Open carpeted areas and circulation routes
  • Entrance lanes and heavily used patches
  • Identified spots and stains for assessment
  • Accessible carpet edges and agreed furniture movement

Separate scope decisions

Furniture relocation, odour investigation, adhesive removal and damaged carpet are separate considerations. Stain removal and restoration of worn fibres cannot be assumed.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Will every stain come out?

Some marks are permanent changes to the fibre or dye. Photograph the affected areas and identify the substance where possible so expectations can be discussed before treatment.

Do we need to move all furniture?

Agree which items will be moved, who will move them and whether cleaning underneath is included. Clear small personal items before access.

What access details help with a Toronto service quote?

Include the property address, permitted work hours, loading arrangements and the areas controlled by tenants or building management. Identify public routes and neighbouring spaces that the proposed work could affect.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
