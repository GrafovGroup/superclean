Graffiti removal in Vaughan

Commercial graffiti removal enquiries in Vaughan. Assess plaza walls, painted doors and shared exteriors with clear surface, permission and access details.

YOUR PROPERTY

Clarify who controls the exterior before treatment.

Define the site

If graffiti affects a leased Vaughan unit, the tenant who notices it may not control the exterior finish. Identify the owner or manager, the affected material and any building-wide coating or appearance requirements. Photograph adjoining unmarked areas so the intended finish is clear.

Access and operations

Describe shared parking, waste collection and customer access around the wall. If several units are involved, list each frontage or service area and the person who can release it for work. A combined request still needs a surface-by-surface assessment.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Vaughan request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Paint, marker or other identified graffiti for assessment
  • Masonry, metal, glass or coated surfaces
  • A discreet test area where appropriate
  • Protection of adjacent surfaces and public access

Separate scope decisions

Residual shadows, damaged finishes, repainting and protective coatings are separate decisions. The result depends on the marking, substrate and any previous treatment.

PREPARE YOUR QUOTE

Send the details that change the plan.

  • Owner or manager contact for shared surfaces
  • Surface material and any protective coating
  • Photographs of adjacent unmarked finishes
  • Access arrangements for every affected unit

Include your full Vaughan address, target dates and site contact. Availability, the method, included tasks and any specialist requirements are confirmed for the property before work is arranged.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can several units in a commercial plaza be reviewed together?

Yes. Label photographs by unit or wall and identify the shared property manager. Confirm the surface types, permissions and access windows for each area rather than assuming one method fits every frontage.

Can removal include a protective coating afterwards?

Request the coating as a separate option. Review compatibility, appearance, future cleaning and the owner’s finish requirements before choosing a system.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
