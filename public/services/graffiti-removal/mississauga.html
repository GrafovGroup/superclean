YOUR PROPERTY

Plan each surface and each access zone.

Define the site

A graffiti enquiry for a Mississauga plaza or industrial property may include a customer-facing sign, painted loading door and exposed concrete wall. Itemise those surfaces rather than providing one combined area. Note vehicle movement and any operations that must remain active near the treatment zone.

Access and operations

Confirm whether a tenant or property manager can authorise the work. For a shared parking or loading area, mark the bays and doors that must stay clear. Describe water access and drainage restrictions so treatment and collection arrangements can be assessed.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Mississauga request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Paint, marker or other identified graffiti for assessment
  • Masonry, metal, glass or coated surfaces
  • A discreet test area where appropriate
  • Protection of adjacent surfaces and public access

Separate scope decisions

Residual shadows, damaged finishes, repainting and protective coatings are separate decisions. The result depends on the marking, substrate and any previous treatment.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can a loading-door marking be treated during business hours?

Share the delivery schedule and whether an alternative door is available. Treatment, drying and vehicle access must be coordinated; daytime work cannot be confirmed from photographs alone.

Should signage and masonry be included in the same request?

They can be listed in one enquiry, but identify them separately. Printed graphics, painted finishes and porous masonry have different treatment constraints and should receive distinct scope decisions.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
