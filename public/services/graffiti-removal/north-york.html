YOUR PROPERTY

Separate the marking from the building finish.

Define the site

A tag on a brick wall, painted service door and storefront window needs three different assessments. For your North York property, photograph each affected material separately. Record previous removal attempts and whether the surface belongs to the tenant, condominium corporation or building owner.

Access and operations

For an entrance or shared walkway, identify the route people need to keep using. Work on a loading-side wall needs a different access window from work beside a customer entrance. Include parking restrictions and the distance between equipment access and the marked surface.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your North York request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Paint, marker or other identified graffiti for assessment
  • Masonry, metal, glass or coated surfaces
  • A discreet test area where appropriate
  • Protection of adjacent surfaces and public access

Separate scope decisions

Residual shadows, damaged finishes, repainting and protective coatings are separate decisions. The result depends on the marking, substrate and any previous treatment.

PREPARE YOUR QUOTE

Send the details that change the plan.

  • Close-up and wider photographs of each marked surface
  • Approximate marked area and height above the ground
  • Paint, marker or other material, if known
  • Owner or manager approval for the affected wall or glazing

Include your full North York address, target dates and site contact. Availability, the method, included tasks and any specialist requirements are confirmed for the property before work is arranged.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can graffiti be assessed on a condominium’s ground-floor exterior?

Yes. Send the building address and photographs, and identify who controls the exterior surface. A tenant’s permission may not cover common property; arrange approval and access through the responsible manager.

What if paint covers both brick and a storefront frame?

List them as separate surfaces. A test area and material-specific method help set expectations; the same treatment should not be assumed suitable for masonry, coated metal and glass.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
