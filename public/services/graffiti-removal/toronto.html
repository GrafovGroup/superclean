YOUR PROPERTY

Account for the public space around the wall.

Define the site

A Toronto storefront or mixed-use building can have very little separation between a marked wall and an active entrance. Show the entire work area, including doors, signs and adjacent finishes, as well as the graffiti itself. Identify fragile or previously restored masonry before discussing a removal method.

Access and operations

Give the property address, opening hours and any shared laneway or loading restrictions. Where the proposed work affects a public route or a neighbouring property, identify the responsible parties and access arrangements before confirming a schedule.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Toronto request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Paint, marker or other identified graffiti for assessment
  • Masonry, metal, glass or coated surfaces
  • A discreet test area where appropriate
  • Protection of adjacent surfaces and public access

Separate scope decisions

Residual shadows, damaged finishes, repainting and protective coatings are separate decisions. The result depends on the marking, substrate and any previous treatment.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can a marked heritage or older masonry surface be assessed?

Provide the material, known repairs and any restrictions imposed by the owner or conservation requirements. Cleaning suitability must be assessed; aggressive pressure or chemicals should not be assumed appropriate.

Does an exterior graffiti quote include repainting?

Ask for repainting or protective coating as a separate item. Removal may leave shadows or reveal existing damage, so the expected finish and any follow-up work need their own agreement.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
