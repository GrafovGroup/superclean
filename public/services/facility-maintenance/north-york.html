YOUR PROPERTY

Turn the asset list into defined work orders.

Define the site

For a North York office, commercial unit or managed property, identify the tenant space and the areas controlled by building management. A suite-level request should name any shared corridors, plant rooms or exterior areas separately. Send an asset list, models, fault descriptions and service history. Mark urgent priorities and distinguish planned maintenance from a specific repair request.

Access and operations

Identify operating hours, shutdown opportunities and tenant access. Group tasks by asset while preserving the approval and return-to-service responsibility for each. List security-desk requirements, elevator access and the person who can release restricted rooms. For multiple floors or units, provide a separate access contact and work window where they differ.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can cleaning and maintenance be coordinated?

List cleaning tasks and asset work separately. Access can be discussed together while keeping qualifications, parts and acceptance requirements clear.

Are replacement parts included automatically?

Identify known parts or specifications where available. Supply, compatibility, labour and approval for additional work should be explicit.

Can a North York tenant arrange work in shared building areas?

Identify which spaces the tenant controls and which require the owner or manager’s approval. A request can include both, but permissions, access contacts and responsibilities must be clear for each area.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
