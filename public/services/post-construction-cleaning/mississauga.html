YOUR PROPERTY

Agree when the site is ready for the final clean.

Define the site

For a Mississauga office fit-out, retail unit or commercial renovation, distinguish a builders’ clean from the final presentation clean. List the rooms released by the trades, the finishes already installed and any areas still under construction. A final clean performed before dusty work ends may need a separate return visit.

Access and operations

Share the handover date, trade completion sequence, elevator or loading access and the person authorised to accept the work. Define who removes construction debris and protective coverings. Glass residue, sensitive flooring and overhead dust require explicit assessment rather than inclusion by assumption.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Mississauga request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Rough-clean tasks after agreed trade milestones
  • Fine dust on accessible surfaces and fixtures
  • Kitchenettes, washrooms and finished common areas
  • Final detailing against an agreed handover checklist

Separate scope decisions

Trade waste, paint or adhesive removal, exterior access and re-cleaning after further construction should be listed separately. Hazardous materials require their own assessment.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can the work be split into a rough clean and a final clean?

Identify the expected condition and released areas at each stage. The proposal should separate visits, trade readiness, tasks and inspection requirements so recontamination does not create an unclear scope.

Does the quote include paint and adhesive removal from new glass?

List residue separately and provide the glazing specifications where available. Removal methods must be compatible with the glass and coatings, and existing scratches or damage should be recorded before work.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
