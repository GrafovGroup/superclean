YOUR PROPERTY

Build the cleaning sequence around the handover.

Define the site

For a North York office, commercial unit or managed property, identify the tenant space and the areas controlled by building management. A suite-level request should name any shared corridors, plant rooms or exterior areas separately. Provide drawings, floor area, finish schedules and the handover checklist. Confirm waste responsibilities, lift bookings, water and power, and who will sign off the completed scope.

Access and operations

Identify when trades finish, utilities become available and the building must be handed over. Additional trade visits after the final clean can create a need for further work. List security-desk requirements, elevator access and the person who can release restricted rooms. For multiple floors or units, provide a separate access contact and work window where they differ.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your North York request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Rough-clean tasks after agreed trade milestones
  • Fine dust on accessible surfaces and fixtures
  • Kitchenettes, washrooms and finished common areas
  • Final detailing against an agreed handover checklist

Separate scope decisions

Trade waste, paint or adhesive removal, exterior access and re-cleaning after further construction should be listed separately. Hazardous materials require their own assessment.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can the work be split into stages?

Include the rough-clean, detail-clean and handover dates you need. Access and trade completion can then be assessed for each stage.

Is removal of builders’ waste included?

State the material, quantity and disposal responsibilities in your enquiry. Bulk waste handling should not be assumed to be part of a final detail clean.

Can a North York tenant arrange work in shared building areas?

Identify which spaces the tenant controls and which require the owner or manager’s approval. A request can include both, but permissions, access contacts and responsibilities must be clear for each area.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
