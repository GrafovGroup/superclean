Define the site

For a Toronto property, distinguish the work area from neighbouring premises, shared entrances and public routes. Provide the site contact who can approve access to each affected area. Send building information, prior surveys, management documents and the specific questions you need resolved. Identify the property owner and any planned changes in use or construction.

Access and operations

Provide renovation dates, procurement deadlines and access to relevant records. Site attendance and document review may require different contacts and work windows. LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can existing reports be reviewed before new work is planned?

Provide the reports, proposed work limits and the decisions they need to support. The specialist can then assess the review scope and information gaps.

Is consulting the same as an abatement contract?

No. Advisory review, material assessment and removal work have different responsibilities and deliverables that should be specified separately.

What access details help with a Toronto service quote?

Include the property address, permitted work hours, loading arrangements and the areas controlled by tenants or building management. Identify public routes and neighbouring spaces that the proposed work could affect.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
