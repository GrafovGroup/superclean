YOUR PROPERTY

Bring overhead surfaces into the facility maintenance plan.

Define the site

For a Toronto property, distinguish the work area from neighbouring premises, shared entrances and public routes. Provide the site contact who can approve access to each affected area. Provide height measurements, photographs, facility drawings and information about the dust. Identify sensitive stock, active machinery and site isolation procedures.

Access and operations

Coordinate production pauses, stock movement and exclusion zones. Working height, floor loading, aisle width and overhead obstructions affect access planning. Include loading or service-elevator bookings, tenant restrictions and any shared laneway access. Give the work-site address even when the request comes from a separate management office.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Toronto request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Accessible beams, ledges and structural surfaces
  • External surfaces of overhead fixtures
  • Elevated vents and pipework within the agreed scope
  • Work zones above aisles and occupied areas

Separate scope decisions

Internal duct cleaning, machinery cleaning, electrical work and combustible or hazardous dust need separate assessment. Do not treat an overhead dusting quote as approval for those tasks.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Does high-level dusting include the inside of ducts?

No scope should assume that. Identify internal ventilation cleaning as a separate requirement, with its own system and access information.

Can work happen above active production?

Describe production activity and contamination concerns. Access, isolation and protection must be agreed before any work around operating equipment.

What access details help with a Toronto service quote?

Include the property address, permitted work hours, loading arrangements and the areas controlled by tenants or building management. Identify public routes and neighbouring spaces that the proposed work could affect.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
