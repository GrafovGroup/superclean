Duct Cleaning

Discuss ventilation duct cleaning for your commercial building.

Request a quote ↗

SERVICE SCOPE

Map the ventilation system before defining the work.

Scope options to discuss

  • Identified supply and return duct sections
  • Accessible grilles, diffusers and registers
  • Agreed debris collection and work-area protection
  • Documentation of accessible sections and exclusions

Agree these items separately

Kitchen grease exhaust, mechanical repairs, balancing, filters, inaccessible sections and suspected hazardous contamination require separate assessment.

Your written quote defines the included tasks, exclusions and responsibilities.

ACCESS & SCHEDULING

Fit the work around your facility.

Timing and access

Agree shutdown responsibility, access to plant rooms and the sequence of occupied spaces. Identify ceilings or tenant areas that require separate building-manager permission.

Before the site assessment

Provide system drawings, approximate duct routes, access panels and recent reports. Describe the concern and distinguish routine accumulated dust from a specific contamination issue.

SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you request a quote.

Does cleaning include balancing or repairing the HVAC system?

List those needs separately. Cleaning accessible duct sections does not define mechanical servicing, airflow testing or system balancing.

What if drawings are unavailable?

Provide the building use, system locations and accessible photographs. A site assessment may be needed to establish the route, access and realistic cleaning scope.
