Define the site

For a North York office, commercial unit or managed property, identify the tenant space and the areas controlled by building management. A suite-level request should name any shared corridors, plant rooms or exterior areas separately. Send washroom and bin counts, existing unit details and preferred service arrangement. Include storage, collection access and responsibility for consumables.

Access and operations

Identify washroom use, opening hours and any privacy or escort requirements. Provide peak occupancy periods and the access available to service each unit. List security-desk requirements, elevator access and the person who can release restricted rooms. For multiple floors or units, provide a separate access contact and work window where they differ.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can existing sanitary bins be used?

Provide the model, condition and current servicing arrangement. Compatibility with the proposed collection or exchange approach needs confirmation.

Is this part of a janitorial contract?

It can be included as a separate requested item. State who supplies units, services them and arranges the relevant waste handling.

Can a North York tenant arrange work in shared building areas?

Identify which spaces the tenant controls and which require the owner or manager’s approval. A request can include both, but permissions, access contacts and responsibilities must be clear for each area.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
