YOUR PROPERTY

Make shared-area responsibilities explicit.

Define the site

For a Vaughan business suite or multi-unit property, distinguish the office interior from shared entrances, washrooms and parking-side access. Identify who arranges common-area cleaning and which tasks your own contract must cover. Reception finishes and customer-facing rooms should be listed rather than assumed.

Access and operations

Provide opening hours, alarm procedures and the on-site contact. For multiple suites, identify separate keys or access approvals and the order in which spaces become available. Seasonal entrance care and periodic floor treatment should be costed separately from routine tasks.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Vaughan request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Reception, meeting rooms and accessible work areas
  • Washrooms, shared kitchens and replenishment responsibilities
  • Vacuuming and care of agreed hard-floor surfaces
  • Waste collection to designated on-site collection points

Separate scope decisions

Carpet extraction, exterior windows, high-level work and consumable supplies should each be identified in the quote. Secure desks, documents and restricted rooms need clear boundaries.

PREPARE YOUR QUOTE

Send the details that change the plan.

  • Opening hours and after-hours approval contact
  • Floor finishes and entrance-mat arrangements
  • Routine tasks versus periodic deep-cleaning priorities

Include your full Vaughan address, target dates and site contact. Availability, the method, included tasks and any specialist requirements are confirmed for the property before work is arranged.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can one proposal cover several business suites?

List each suite, area and access window, together with the tasks under the tenant’s or manager’s control. A shared proposal can then keep each suite’s scope and responsibilities explicit.

Are entrance mats and floor restoration included?

Ask for the required mat care and periodic floor work separately. Daily vacuuming or mopping does not automatically include extraction, stripping, refinishing or replacement.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
