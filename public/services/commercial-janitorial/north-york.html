Office cleaning in North York

Commercial janitorial and office cleaning in North York. Plan recurring tasks, shared spaces, after-hours access and periodic floor care for your facility.

YOUR PROPERTY

Give each floor and tenant a clear cleaning boundary.

Define the site

A North York office enquiry should separate the leased suite from shared corridors, elevators and washrooms. List which areas your organisation controls, where building staff already provide service, and any rooms with restricted access. This prevents two proposals from pricing different responsibilities.

Access and operations

For evening cleaning, provide the staff departure time, security-desk procedure and the latest lock-up time. If people continue working, identify occupied zones and tasks that could interrupt them. Reception areas and washrooms may need a different frequency from meeting rooms.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your North York request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Reception, meeting rooms and accessible work areas
  • Washrooms, shared kitchens and replenishment responsibilities
  • Vacuuming and care of agreed hard-floor surfaces
  • Waste collection to designated on-site collection points

Separate scope decisions

Carpet extraction, exterior windows, high-level work and consumable supplies should each be identified in the quote. Secure desks, documents and restricted rooms need clear boundaries.

PREPARE YOUR QUOTE

Send the details that change the plan.

  • Cleanable floor area and number of suites or floors
  • Tenant versus landlord-maintained areas
  • Washroom, kitchenette and waste-room responsibilities
  • Alarm, security and after-hours access procedures

Include your full North York address, target dates and site contact. Availability, the method, included tasks and any specialist requirements are confirmed for the property before work is arranged.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can we request cleaning for one suite in a larger building?

Yes. Define the suite boundary and any shared areas separately. Include the building manager’s access rules and clarify whether waste must be taken to a designated common collection point.

Can night cleaning be included in the scope?

Provide the available entry and lock-up window, occupancy and tasks. The quote should state the agreed timing, access responsibilities and any work that must be completed outside that window.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
