Define the site

For a Vaughan commercial unit or managed property, distinguish the tenant’s work area from shared parking, entrances and service spaces. LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can several appliances be listed together?

Identify which appliance connects to each flue and provide separate records where possible. Shared systems and different fuels need individual scope review.

Does cleaning resolve a damaged liner or draught issue?

Those concerns need separate assessment. Describe the symptoms and existing reports before assuming that residue removal alone will address them.

Can several Vaughan commercial units be included in one enquiry?

List each unit, task and access contact, including the manager responsible for shared areas. A combined request should preserve the individual scope, permissions and availability assessment for every property.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
