YOUR PROPERTY

Give workout spaces and changing areas their own routines.

Define the site

For a Mississauga business property, separate public and office areas from storage, loading and active operations. Include a site plan or labelled photographs so the proposed tasks have a clear boundary. Send opening hours, class schedules, floor types and changing-room counts. Identify equipment care instructions, member access routes and responsibility for consumables.

Access and operations

Map class changeovers, peak attendance and closing hours. Wet areas and heavily used touchpoints may need a different frequency from offices or storage rooms. Share shift changes, delivery windows and vehicle routes through the site. Name the person responsible for clearing the work area and confirm which operations must remain accessible.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Mississauga request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Agreed equipment touchpoints and accessible surfaces
  • Waste collection and replenishment responsibilities

Separate scope decisions

Equipment servicing, pool systems, specialist flooring restoration and high-level cleaning require separate scopes. Cleaning products must be compatible with equipment finishes.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Will exercise equipment be moved or dismantled?

Identify any movement in advance and follow the equipment manufacturer’s requirements. Internal servicing and dismantling should be handled as separate tasks.

Can cleaning fit around classes?

Share the timetable and the minimum time between sessions. Access and the sequence of work can then be assessed for each studio.

Can work be planned around deliveries at a Mississauga facility?

Provide delivery times, loading-door locations and the route to the proposed work area. Scope and timing can then be assessed around the areas the operator can release; no access arrangement is assumed in advance.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
