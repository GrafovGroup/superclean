YOUR PROPERTY

A practical handover scope for property managers.

Define the site

For a Mississauga business property, separate public and office areas from storage, loading and active operations. Include a site plan or labelled photographs so the proposed tasks have a clear boundary. Share unit count, approximate size, condition photos and your move-in checklist. Include key collection, parking, elevator bookings and the property manager’s handover contact.

Access and operations

Work back from the next tenant’s access date. Confirm that trades and move-out activity are complete before the final clean, and allow for any agreed drying time. Share shift changes, delivery windows and vehicle routes through the site. Name the person responsible for clearing the work area and confirm which operations must remain accessible.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your Mississauga request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Vacant-unit rooms and accessible surfaces
  • Kitchens and bathrooms as specified
  • Floor care matched to the existing finishes
  • Shared entrances or amenities when separately listed

Separate scope decisions

Tenant belongings, junk removal, pests, hazardous materials, repairs and restoration require separate assessment. Appliances, cupboards, balconies and interior windows should be itemised.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Is this intended for property portfolios?

This enquiry is for landlords and property managers arranging vacant-unit turnovers or common-area cleaning in professionally managed properties.

Are appliance interiors included?

List ovens, refrigerators and other appliances individually, along with their condition and whether they will be empty and accessible.

Can work be planned around deliveries at a Mississauga facility?

Provide delivery times, loading-door locations and the route to the proposed work area. Scope and timing can then be assessed around the areas the operator can release; no access arrangement is assumed in advance.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
