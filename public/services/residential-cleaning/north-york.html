YOUR PROPERTY

A practical handover scope for property managers.

Define the site

For a North York office, commercial unit or managed property, identify the tenant space and the areas controlled by building management. A suite-level request should name any shared corridors, plant rooms or exterior areas separately. Share unit count, approximate size, condition photos and your move-in checklist. Include key collection, parking, elevator bookings and the property manager’s handover contact.

Access and operations

Work back from the next tenant’s access date. Confirm that trades and move-out activity are complete before the final clean, and allow for any agreed drying time. List security-desk requirements, elevator access and the person who can release restricted rooms. For multiple floors or units, provide a separate access contact and work window where they differ.

SERVICE SCOPE

What to include in your North York request.

Tasks to discuss

  • Vacant-unit rooms and accessible surfaces
  • Kitchens and bathrooms as specified
  • Floor care matched to the existing finishes
  • Shared entrances or amenities when separately listed

Separate scope decisions

Tenant belongings, junk removal, pests, hazardous materials, repairs and restoration require separate assessment. Appliances, cupboards, balconies and interior windows should be itemised.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Is this intended for property portfolios?

This enquiry is for landlords and property managers arranging vacant-unit turnovers or common-area cleaning in professionally managed properties.

Are appliance interiors included?

List ovens, refrigerators and other appliances individually, along with their condition and whether they will be empty and accessible.

Can a North York tenant arrange work in shared building areas?

Identify which spaces the tenant controls and which require the owner or manager’s approval. A request can include both, but permissions, access contacts and responsibilities must be clear for each area.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
