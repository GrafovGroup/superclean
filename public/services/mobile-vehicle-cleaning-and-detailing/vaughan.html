Define the site

For a Vaughan commercial unit or managed property, distinguish the tenant’s work area from shared parking, entrances and service spaces. Identify the owner or manager responsible for common property before requesting a combined scope. Send vehicle types, fleet count, condition photographs and interior or exterior priorities. Identify wraps, sensitive equipment, water restrictions and the person releasing each vehicle.

Access and operations

List vehicles by departure time and parking position. Agree the route between vehicles, water arrangements, keys and the time each unit must return to service. Label the units or work zones involved and list their access windows. Shared parking, waste collection and customer routes should be considered alongside the site’s operating hours.

LOCAL SERVICE QUESTIONS

Before you arrange the work.

Can different vehicle types be included in one fleet request?

Yes. Group vehicles by type and condition, with a separate checklist for each group. A delivery van, passenger shuttle and work truck may need different time and equipment.

Does a mobile wash include cargo-area treatment?

List the cargo, residue and required result separately. A cab-detailing or exterior-wash quote does not define specialist cargo-area cleaning.

Can several Vaughan commercial units be included in one enquiry?

List each unit, task and access contact, including the manager responsible for shared areas. A combined request should preserve the individual scope, permissions and availability assessment for every property.

What determines the quote?

The affected area, surface or equipment condition, access, work window and agreed tasks determine the scope. Send the site details above; pricing and scheduling are confirmed after review.
