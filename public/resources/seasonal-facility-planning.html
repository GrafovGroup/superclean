FACILITY NOTES

Plan a seasonal facility clean

Consider entrances, exterior surfaces and scheduling when seasonal conditions change.

PRACTICAL CONSIDERATIONS

A clearer starting point.

Entrance care

Review matting, moisture and tracked-in debris. Plan cleaning around actual footfall rather than assuming the same routine works year-round.

Exterior access

Discuss surface condition, drainage, pedestrian traffic and suitable weather windows before arranging washing or glass care.

Coordinate the calendar

Identify busy periods, closures and maintenance work so cleaning does not conflict with other building activities.
