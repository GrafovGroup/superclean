Plan around your Vaughan property.

USEFUL ANSWERS

Your Vaughan questions.

Who approves work on a shared exterior?

Identify the property owner or manager responsible for the surface, even if a tenant reports the issue. Permission, access and responsibility for the final result should be clear before work is arranged.

What do I need for a Vaughan quote?

Include the Vaughan property address, floor area, facility type, access hours and desired tasks. Add photos or an existing checklist where available.

Can I request supporting documentation?

Ask for the credentials, insurance evidence, method information and references relevant to your proposed work. Any requirements should be confirmed before agreement.
