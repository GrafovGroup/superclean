QUOTE ENQUIRIES

How your information is handled.

Information you provide

The form collects your company, contact details, city and service requirements. You can email relevant photos separately. Superclean uses this information to review and respond to your enquiry.

Form processing

Submissions are sent directly to Formspree, our form-processing service, for storage and delivery to Superclean. Formspree also processes technical information for spam prevention. Read Formspree’s privacy policy for details about its handling of information.

Photos and retention

Include only photos relevant to the proposed work. Avoid personal documents or other unnecessary sensitive information. Form enquiries are handled through the business Formspree account and email system. Photos sent by email are handled through the email system. Contact get@superclean.ca about access, correction, retention or deletion of your enquiry information.

Contact preferences

Submitting a request asks Superclean to respond about that enquiry. It does not subscribe you to promotional messages. Include your phone number only if you want a phone response.
